About

The name of the planning laboratory is derived from UrBAn Systems, LANd Use, ReGional, Public WorKs, And TranSport.

BALANGKAS is a permanent research and graduate-mentorship program at the Department of Community and Environmental Resource Planning (DCERP), College of Human Ecology, University of the Philippines Los Baños.

Rationale

The BALANGKAS Planning Laboratory is a permanent research and graduate mentorship program addressing land use, transportation, regional, and public works constraints in the CALABARZON growth area. It uses publicly accessible secondary datasets and free GIS tools to compile, harmonize, and analyze existing public geospatial data into reusable decision-support products like suitability, transport accessibility, and hazard exposure layers. This approach ensures low cost, rapid execution, reproducibility, and a shared spatial data infrastructure for future graduate cohorts.

Objectives

  1. Design, implement, and evaluate research, mentorship, and innovation activities in land use, transport, regional, and public-works planning using openly available secondary geospatial datasets.
  2. Facilitate graduate research apprenticeship and hands-on GIS/QGIS training, with each mentee owning a thematic sub-study nested in a shared spatial database.
  3. Initiate and sustain collaboration with LGUs, DHSUD, SUCs/HEIs, and partner institutions on land-use-mapping–aligned planning analytics.
  4. Conduct extension work translating spatial decision-support products into policy briefs and LGU planning inputs for public service.
